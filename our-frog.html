Poem

Our Frog

By Ema Kimmage

Unbothered, slimy, and green,

The calmest creature you've ever seen,

Sitting on a rock without a plan,

Living better than most humans can.

No alarm. No agenda. No dread.

Just a lily pad, a fly, a head.

Jumps when ready, lands where it lands,

Asks nothing of its little hands,

Sleeps when tired, wakes when not,

Perfectly content with what it's got.
