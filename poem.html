Poem

Spring at Maret

By Maya Swanson

At long last the snow banks from past winter storms

Are melting beneath all the sun rays so warm

After months bundled up in a warm, cozy den

Our favorite mascot emerges again

Blinking, his green eyes adjust to the light

Part of him misses the long winter's night

But this frog has a duty, for there is a pond

That needs protecting, so he stifles his yawn

Besides, he thinks, there's no time to be tired

When the front lawn of Woodley remains unadmired

For the grass and the trees are both greener than ever

As an English class reads, enjoying the weather

He walks around Woodley, where flowers are blooming

As lunch eaters run from the bumble bees looming

Shouts carry up from the turf field below

As friends find lacrosse, base, and soft balls to throw

The frog knows the best times have not happened yet

For soon will come field day, spring fling, and the fête

Our mascot's green heart beats happily in his chest

For spring at Maret is always just the best
