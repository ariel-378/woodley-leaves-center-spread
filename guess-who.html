Guess Who

On a rainy April morning…

By Ema Kimmage

On a rainy April morning

My cereal was frozen cold,

Shampoo exploded without warning,

Alright, am I really getting trolled?

It only worsened as the day went on —

I found my stapler in the jell-o,

And mashed potatoes in my lawn.

It just can't be a coincidence, I bellow!

Then, I was carried by a rainbow horse

To find a message in the pool

It's the first day of — why, of course…

Reveal the answer
I'm the April Fool!
